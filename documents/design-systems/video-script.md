# Design Systems video script

Target: under 60 seconds. ~145 words reads at about 55 seconds with beats.
Marketer language, no jargon until "tokens" is earned. [beat] = short pause.

---

How many versions of the same button are on your website right now?

[beat]

Most websites were never designed. They were accumulated. Every campaign
leaves something behind: a slightly different hero, a new blue, one more
button style. None of it is wrong on its own. But eventually every new page
needs a designer, and your marketing ships at the speed of the review
queue.

We fix that with something I call a design dictionary. Every design
decision your brand makes, colors, type, spacing, buttons, gets made once
and stored as code. Components are built from those decisions, so nothing
can drift off brand. And your team composes pages from ready-made sections
and publishes in minutes. No mockup, no ticket, no developer.

Change a brand color once, and every page updates itself.

[beat]

Nobody has to have an opinion about the button ever again.

See it live at ohmni tech slash design.

---

Delivery notes: the opening question wants a real pause; let them count.
"Accumulated" is the word to lean on. The button line is the smile beat.
End on the URL, said slowly.
