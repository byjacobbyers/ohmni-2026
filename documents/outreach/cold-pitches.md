# Cold pitches, 20 variants, short form

Ranked best first. Ranking logic: a hard verifiable number beats a metaphor,
a visceral specific beats a category claim, and pain beats positioning. The
top tier leads with something the prospect can check or feel; the bottom tier
is positioning, useful for later touches in a sequence, weak as an opener.
Voice rules held: no em dashes, no invented numbers, no "Quick question."

FirstName and CompanyName are merge fields. CTAs swap freely.
